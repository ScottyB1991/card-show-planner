Privacy Policy

The Card Map — Last updated: 1 October 2026

The Card Map is a UK card-show discovery and planning service designed to help collectors find events, plan trips, save shows and keep track of events they have attended.

Information you provide

If you create an account, The Card Map uses your email address and authentication information to create and secure your account.

When signed in, you may choose to save information including:

  • card shows you have saved;
  • whether a show is marked as interested, going or attended;
  • favourite shows;
  • notes you add to saved shows; and
  • your selected card and collecting interests.

This information is used to provide The Card Map's account, planning, personalisation and Collector Journey features.

Location

The Card Map includes an optional location feature to help sort card shows by distance.

Location is requested only when you choose to use this feature. The Card Map uses location supplied by your device to calculate approximate distances to shows.

You may instead enter a town or postcode area. This information may be stored locally on your device so that your chosen area can be remembered. Location access is not required to browse card shows.

How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • provide and maintain The Card Map;
  • authenticate users;
  • save show plans and preferences;
  • personalise relevant card-show features;
  • provide distance and trip-planning features; and
  • maintain the security and reliability of the service.

The Card Map does not sell your personal information.

Service providers

The Card Map uses third-party services to provide parts of the service. Supabase provides database and account authentication services.

Map content may be provided using OpenStreetMap and related mapping technology. Links to external mapping, ticketing or organiser websites may open third-party services, which operate under their own privacy policies.

Local storage

The Card Map may store some preferences locally on your device, such as your chosen town or postcode area and technical settings required for the service to operate.

Data security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information transmitted between The Card Map and its service providers. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

Location access is optional and can be declined or controlled through your device settings. You can use the main card-show discovery features without providing your device location. You can also sign out of your Card Map account at any time.

Data retention and account deletion

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and meet applicable legal, security or operational requirements.

Account holders can request deletion of their Card Map account and associated personal information. The Card Map also provides account deletion controls for registered users.

Children's privacy

The Card Map is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where required by law.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated as The Card Map develops. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, account deletion requests or other data-related enquiries, please use the contact details provided through The Card Map.
