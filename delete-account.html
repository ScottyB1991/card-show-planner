Delete your The Card Map account

The Card Map allows registered users to request deletion of their account and associated account data.

How to delete your account

Open The Card Map and sign in to the account you want to delete. Go to your account settings and choose the option to delete your account. Follow the confirmation steps shown in the app.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, your account and associated user data, including saved events and account preferences, are deleted.

If you need assistance with an account deletion request, contact The Card Map using the support contact provided in the app.
